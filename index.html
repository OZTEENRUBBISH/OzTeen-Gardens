Who we are

Started by a local, still run that way.

OzTeen started with one ute and a phone number, clearing junk for neighbours around the Northern Beaches. The name stuck, the ute got busier, and the promise never changed: turn up when we say, quote it straight and do the work ourselves.

Gardens & Landscaping grew from the same idea. Now there's an excavator, garden design and regular garden care, with no call centre and no subcontractors you've never met. If you speak to us on the phone, we're the ones showing up at your door.
